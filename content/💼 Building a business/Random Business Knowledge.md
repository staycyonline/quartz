[5 Step process on how to start a business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nIk3DedjxJM)
---

#### What? - Businesses are built from either
 - pain - a big problem for many - develop a system to make it easier
 - profession - do something for sometime and start a private practice - teach others how to do
 - passion - passionate about something

#### Who? - How to make it a business - get the bad idea out fast - iterate on feedback and make it better
-  People like you - very personal way
-  People you Helped before
-  Underserved people - very formal way

	Define the target - Age Gender profession Problem Interests

More specific i get i can charge more !

#### How? - how can you increase the upsides more than others or reduce the bad stuff significantly
- Upside
	- How does my thing make it Easier
	- How does my thing  something Faster
	- How does my thing Guarantee success
	- How does my thing get Immediate results
	- How does my thing help Experience the dream of customer

- Bad stuff
	- Things that slow them down
	- Reduce risks of doing something
	- Things that cause pain, suffer and damage

- Ask people who you are going to sell what they want will give ideas about upside, bad stuff

#### Combine
 - I help ***Who***  get ***Upside*** without ***Downside***  through ***Unique Mechanism***

#### Unique Mechanism
- Unique mechanism can only be achieved after starting out.
- List (unordered) or Steps(ordered) to achieve - name it - it becomes your propriety property
